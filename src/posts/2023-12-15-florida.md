As November became December I traveled to Florida from Wisconsin to visit my brother and run a half marathon.

The last race I ran was in early October and I didn't take any kind of a break in training afterwards - in hope of stacking "gains". I thought I could really show off in Florida this way.

Instead, it set the encroaching plantar fasciitis in my left foot into a state of injury and pain. I stopped training then, but the damage had been done. I hobbled out of bed each morning and made an appointment with a podiatrist. The doctor said I should be just fine, but in order the heal, I'd need to dial back the running, do lots of stretching, get some orthotics and wear a plastic boot to bed. It would probably be best to stop running all together but the ticket had been bought and the race was registered for. I would focus on healing after Florida.

This trip wasn't just about running anyway, I was looking forward to a long overdue visit to my brother and his wife. Generously, they would provide me a place to stay during the 5 days I'd be there.

The night I arrived I abandoned the idea of immediately doing a shake out run, delays had set my arrival later than I'd planned. But the next morning I set out in the dark at 6am. I passed a few other runners. Remarkably, one was wearing a knit hat and another was in a full sweatsuit. It was humid and in the low 70s outside. This was another planet and its inhabitants bore little resemblance to denizens of Wisconsin. 

Later in the morning my brother and I decide we needed to touch a manatee. He'd only ever seen one at a distance and I've never seen one at all.

[Manatees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manatee), sometimes called sea cows, are marine mammals that resemble seals but chunkier. In fact, they look a lot like a macro version of [tardigrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tardigrade). Florida is one of the few places in the world to see them. Some brief research confirmed that it was now manatee viewing season and we need only make our way to some brackish water linked to the Gulf of Mexico - not far from where we were.

My brother navigated us towards a park but we ended up lost near some boat storage. That's when we asked a man that looked like Santa Claus for help. Florida Santa was retired and happy to chat with use after removing his Air Pods.
